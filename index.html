About the numbers

Consumer prices are HICP for European countries and CPI-U for the US. Wages are economy-wide average gross earnings, in nominal terms. Housing is the residential property price index for existing and new dwellings — purchase prices, not rents.

The mortgage line prices the same house at today's rates: it multiplies the change in house prices by the change in the monthly annuity payment, assuming an unchanged loan-to-value ratio and a 25-year repayment mortgage at the country's typical rate for new housing loans. Deposit and fees scale with the price; tax relief, term changes and fixation periods are not modelled.

Sources to refresh against: Eurostat prc_hicp_aind (prices), prc_hpi_a (housing), lc_lci_r2_a (labour costs); OECD Average annual wages; US BLS CPI and Average Hourly Earnings; S&P Cotality Case-Shiller; ECB MIR and national central banks for mortgage rates.

Figures marked ≈ in the table are estimates compiled from published annual rates and are indicative only. Mortgage rates are estimates for every country. Run node scripts/fetch-eurostat.mjs to replace the price, pay and housing figures with live Eurostat data.
