The data, year by year.

Every annual series behind the chart, in the unit its publisher used and as an index rebased to 2016 = 100. That base year is just how the numbers are stored — the calculator rebases them to whichever year you pick. Where a line has no series, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Reading these tables

As published is the number the publisher put out, in their unit — a percentage change, an index on their own base year, or a price in cash. Index is that series rebased so 2016 = 100, which is the only form the chart and the calculator use.

A year marked partial was averaged from fewer periods than a full year — usually the current year, which is not finished. A series marked suspect has a value that contradicts other reporting; it is left in and flagged rather than quietly corrected.

Full provenance for each dataset is on the sources page.
